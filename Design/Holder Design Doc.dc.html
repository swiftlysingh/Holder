HOLDER · DESIGN DOC · v1
The offline card vault,
redesigned.
Holder keeps payment cards, loyalty cards and identity documents encrypted in the iOS Keychain — nothing ever leaves the device. This document defines the redesign: the Emerald Mint theme world, the Pass Deck layout grammar, and the interaction model. Working mocks live in Holder Directions.
01 · Principles
The card is the interface
No list rows describing cards — the card art itself is the row, the detail view, and the copy surface. Fields on the card are the tap targets.
One action per screen
Exactly one saturated element at a time: the floating +, or the active reveal. Everything else is ink, sage, and card art. Secondary actions are quiet text.
Trust is ambient
Keychain + offline messaging lives in footers, the lock screen, and masked-by-default fields — never a marketing splash. Widgets show state, not data.

04 · Layout grammar — deck + sheet
+
Deck: cards peek at 48px each, widening toward the front — 5–7 stay scannable; tap brings a card to the sheet.

Sheet: the focused card rides an elevated sheet; its fields are the actions. Pull down to shuffle back.

Floating +: liquid-glass button, bottom right — the only add entry point. Header carries only a quiet ⚙ settings glyph; search lives as a chip in the filter row.
05 · Interaction model
Tap a field to copy. Number, expiry, holder — each field on the card is its own copy target; a toast confirms ("Number copied"). No separate button row.
Flip for CVV. Tap the card face to flip; CVV lives on the back, masked until flipped, re-masks on flip-back.
Reveal timer. Sensitive reveals auto-expire after 12s; the countdown is visible.
Swipe to archive on deck cards; long-press to reorder; drag to favorites.
Face ID gate on launch and after 60s idle; documents re-gate individually.
Haptics (light impact) on copy, flip, and reveal-expiry.
06 · Cards vs documents
Two item classes with different anatomies:

Cards (payment, loyalty, travel) — rendered card art, copyable fields, flip for back. No photo attachments: the render is the card.

Documents (licence, passport, insurance) — photo-first: front/back capture slots for verification uploads, plus typed fields (number, expiry). Masked behind an extra Face ID gate.

This split is why the payment-card sheet has no attachments strip — photos belong to documents only.
07 · Widgets — state, never data
Small: default card, masked (•• 51006), brand emerald. Tap → Face ID → card.
Medium: 2×2 quick grid, content-palette tiles, masked tails. Tap a tile → Face ID → copy.
Lock screen: circular glyph + inline default card on green-black.
Rule: a widget never shows a full number, CVV, or document photo — it deep-links, the app reveals.
