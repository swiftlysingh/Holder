# Holder Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

Holder stores payment-card details for your use. This policy explains what remains in Apple-managed storage and what limited usage data is sent to service providers.

## Card data

Card numbers, security codes, expiration dates, cardholder names, descriptions, card networks, card images, and card identifiers are not sent to Holder's analytics or purchase providers.

Card records are stored in the Apple Keychain with iCloud synchronization enabled. If you add an image for an “Other Card,” the image is stored in your iCloud container. Widgets receive a local App Group copy containing the card's display name, last four digits, type, network, and identifier. Apple controls the security and synchronization of iCloud and Keychain data under your Apple account settings.

## Usage analytics

Holder uses [PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy) for pseudonymous product analytics. Events may describe app lifecycle, onboarding, fixed screen names, card workflow actions such as save or archive success, scanner usage, widget opens, and paywall or purchase outcomes. These events do not contain stored card content or card categories.

PostHog may also receive an anonymous identifier and technical context such as app version, device model or type, operating-system version, locale, time zone, screen size, network connection type, and IP address. Holder does not call PostHog's user-identification API. Session replay, element interaction capture, surveys, and automatic screen discovery are disabled.

## Purchases

Holder uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) to provide optional in-app purchases. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app-user identifier, product and offering identifiers, purchase status, and entitlement status. Apple processes payment details; Holder does not receive your payment-card information from Apple or RevenueCat.

## Sharing and retention

Holder does not sell personal information. Usage and purchase data is shared only with the providers described above to operate and improve the app. Those providers retain and process data according to their policies.

## Changes and contact

This policy may change when Holder's storage, analytics, or purchase behavior changes. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to [sayhi@swiftlysingh.com](mailto:sayhi@swiftlysingh.com).
